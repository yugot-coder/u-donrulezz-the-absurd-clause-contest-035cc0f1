The Absurd Clause Contest (u-donrulezz-the-absurd-clause-contest-035cc0f1)

One line, 200 characters at most: an absurd clause hidden deep inside a
terms-of-service agreement. Entrants write a single original English sentence
of no more than 200 characters, phrased as a clause that could sit buried deep
inside a terms-of-service agreement: deadpan legal boilerplate that quietly
turns absurd. A judge model compares the clauses in pairs, working from a
brief published with the bounty, and the funniest clause wins.

Judge brief (most important first):
1. Is an absurd clause that could hide deep inside a terms-of-service
   agreement.
2. Is funny on its own, with a joke that lands on one reading and needs no
   setup from outside the line.
3. Sounds like real legal boilerplate until the absurdity hits, so the joke
   comes from the register as much as from the words.
4. Is original and specific, with a concrete absurd image rather than a
   vague shrug at the genre.
5. Is tight and well written, one clean sentence with no wasted words and
   no explaining of its own joke.
